Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How to book a hotel online with Indikosh Travels?

Booking a hotel online is easy through Indikosh Travels. All you need to do first is to download our app on your Android or iOS device or simply use your computer. On the app, tap on the Hotels section and enter the details of the city, the area or the hotel. Fill out the check-in and check-out dates, along with the other details and tap Search. Use the Sort & Filter options so that you can book one as per your convenience.

Q. How to find the best hotels near me?

If you are travelling to a city for the first time and are looking for hotels in the best areas, it is easy to find them on our website and our app. Once you have hit the Search option, you will find a list of all the hotels near the city’s major business districts, tourist areas and transportation hubs. You can even find out which of the areas are ideal for couples and families.
